I’m a Senior Product Designer at CBC Digital Products and Innovation, where I help shape digital experiences used by millions of Canadians. I specialize in turning complex problems into intuitive, human centred products by combining research, strategy, and design craft.

My work spans the entire product lifecycle, from uncovering user needs and defining opportunities to prototyping, high fidelity design, and delivery. I collaborate closely with cross functional teams to create experiences that are accessible, engaging, and aligned with both user and business goals.

Having worked across a broad range of digital disciplines and technologies, I’m passionate about understanding human behaviour and designing products that feel effortless and meaningful. I’m particularly interested in personalization, habit forming experiences, and the evolving role of design in helping people connect with information and each other.
